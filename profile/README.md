## Who We Are

**Ironji Trade Ltd.** is Rwanda's premier transport logistics company, operating since 2018 from Kigali. We provide seamless on-demand transport and distribution of goods anywhere in Rwanda — making the movement of goods simple, fast, reliable, and stress-free.

## Our Mission

> To provide seamless on-demand transport and distribution of goods anywhere in Rwanda, making the movement of goods simple, fast, reliable and seamless.

## What We Do

| Service | Description |
|---|---|
| **Products Distribution** | Factory/warehouse to retailers nationwide — full truckload or less-than-truckload, multi-stop routes, scheduled or on-demand, covering all 30 districts of Rwanda |
| **Inter-Warehouse Transport** | Moving inventory between storage facilities with dedicated trucks, loading/unloading assistance, and climate-controlled options — up to 30 tons per truck |
| **On-Demand Trucking** | Instant truck booking with 30-minute dispatch, any cargo type, flexible truck sizes from pickups to flatbeds |
| **Logistics Planning** | Strategic distribution planning to multiple locations |

## Our Story

Ironji Trade Ltd. was founded in **2018** in Kigali, Rwanda, with a clear vision: to revolutionize how goods move across Rwanda. What started as a transport company has grown into a full-service logistics platform — combining a network of trusted truck owner-drivers with purpose-built technology to deliver reliability, speed, and transparency at every step.

Today, we serve manufacturers, agribusinesses, construction firms, and retailers across all 30 districts of Rwanda, and we're just getting started.
